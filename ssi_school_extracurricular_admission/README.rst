==================================
School Extracurricular - Admission
==================================

Glue module that lets an Extracurricular Participant's fee be billed against a
``school_admission`` payment term instead of only a ``school_enrollment``
payment term. Adds ``admission`` as a Billing Mode on the Offering and the
Participant, an Admission Allocation line pointing to an admission payment
term, and an Extracurricular addendum on the admission payment term itself
so the fee is folded into its totals and invoiced together with the
admission's own payment template.
